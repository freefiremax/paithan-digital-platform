# Policy review — 31 October 2026

## Published changes

All six policy routes now use readable sections, an on-page contents list, a revision date and locale-preserving navigation. English, Marathi and Hindi have matching coverage. Auth forms link to privacy and terms. The chatbot ingestion adapter reads this same published policy content.

The privacy notice describes actual account storage, Google profile/account data, civic submissions, consent-gated aggregate metrics in Neon, authentication/security cookies, provider processing, chatbot limitations, requests and retention uncertainty. Terms distinguish citizen accounts from authorised council roles and do not promise email recovery or online payments that are not configured. Ancillary pages cover rights in third-party photographs, external links, uncertain tourism information and accessibility assistance without claiming certification.

## Primary sources checked

- [GIGW 3.0 guidelines](https://guidelines.india.gov.in/guidelines/) — scope of citizen-facing policies, ownership, privacy and accessibility. Used to select topics, not to claim this portal is certified.
- [NIC website policies](https://guidelines.india.gov.in/website-policies/) — examples of separate terms, privacy, hyperlinking and copyright policies. Their organisation-specific wording was not copied into our portal.
- [MeitY DPDP Rules 2025 publication](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa) and [official commencement notification, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) — commencement is phased. Avoid implying every provision is already operative or that a notice alone establishes compliance.
- [Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) — link the security provider notice; distinguish security processing from optional analytics.
- [Google Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) — data handling varies with service plan; do not promise that chatbot content is never processed for provider improvement. The current provider age and regional restrictions also need an operational review before expanding chatbot audiences.
- [W3C pause/stop/hide guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html) — retain a small pause/resume control for the automatic photo sequence. There are no navigation arrows or thumbnail strip.
